Customer Support FAQ

Q: What are your customer support hours?
A: Customer support is available Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM IST.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: Customers can contact support at support@technova.example.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days after an order is confirmed.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Contact customer support with your order number.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Select Forgot Password on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

Q: How can I request a refund?
A: Contact customer support with your order number and the reason for the refund. Refund eligibility depends on the company's refund policy.
