Refund and Return Policy

1. Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery.
2. Products must be unused and returned with their original packaging.
3. Refund requests must include a valid order number.
4. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
5. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the company shipped an incorrect or damaged item.
6. Digital products are non-refundable after they have been downloaded.
7. Customers should contact support if their item arrives damaged.
